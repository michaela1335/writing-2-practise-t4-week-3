New Zealand Curriculum | Modern Day Narrative Assessment

Task: The Unexpected Text

Genre: Modern Day Fiction

Required Sentence Starter: Begin your story directly from this starter:

"He looked down at his buzzing phone, realizing the text message wasn't meant for him..."

Success Criteria (Target: ~250 words)

  • Figurative Language: Incorporate vivid imagery (similes, metaphors, or sensory action verbs).
  • Writer's Toolbox Sentence Starters: Vary sentence openings (-ing words, -ly adverbs, prepositional phrases, short sentences).
  • SPaG & Paragraphing: Use dialogue punctuation, clear paragraphing, commas, and complex conjunctions (e.g., although, because, while, whenever).
